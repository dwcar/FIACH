# Licensing and provenance

This package is a MATLAB/SPM implementation derived from the algorithms in the
FIACH R package by Tierney and colleagues:

https://github.com/tierneytim/FIACH

The legacy MATLAB files under `legacy_reference` are retained for algorithmic
provenance and are not needed to run the new implementation. SPM12 and MATLAB
are external dependencies and are not redistributed here.

No new distribution licence has been asserted for this assembled private
package. Before distributing it outside the authorised private research group,
review the upstream FIACH licence, the provenance of the legacy MATLAB files,
SPM/MATLAB terms, and the governance terms for the included fMRI data.
